Conference paperIEOM · Cape Town · May 2026

Cold Chain Logistics Performance in Saudi Arabia: Evidence from the Pharmaceutical Industry

Leen AlAbsi, Manar SharafAldeen, Ghalia AlHazmi, Yasmina Sagga and Talah Alzuabi

Published in the Proceedings of the 7th African International Conference on Industrial Engineering and Operations Management, Cape Town, 13–15 May 2026. The study compares two organizations using interviews and data logger records. Both systems work well, while extreme heat, door openings, equipment faults and human error remain the main risks. The paper recommends predictive monitoring, in-transit tracking and backup power.
